The Abbot Samson

AJC While the abbacy was vacant, the prior above all strove to maintain peace in the manstery and to preserve the honor of the church by accepting guests, to disturb no-one willingly, to provoke no-one to anger, to keep everyone and everything in peace. Yet he ignored some matters that should've been corrected about our officials, and especially about the sacristan, as he didn't care what he did about the sacristy, he had not paid any debt or built anything and during the time in which the abbacy was vacant - but the offerings and revenues were foolishly wasted.

AJC And from this, when the chapter was assembled, the prior seemed blameworthy to most of them and was called remiss.

AJC And our brothers reminded each other of this when it came to making the choice of an abbot.

AJC Our cellarer accepted all guests, whatever their condition might be, at the monastery's expense.

AJC Willelm, the sacristan, gave and paid out on his own inititiative; a bountiful man, he gave what should be given and what should not, blinding his eyes with everyone's bribes.

AJC Samson, the under-sacristan, the overseer of the workers, left nothing broken, nothing torn up, nothing split, nothing unrepaired if he could help it; and from this he endeared himself to the monastery and particularly to the cloistered monks in gratitude.
